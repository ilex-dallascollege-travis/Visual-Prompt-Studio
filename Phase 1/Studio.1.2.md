1. What are the lines, sizes, and values doing in the final album cover to guide
our attention or imply depth across the composition?

The strong vertical lines of the far away vampire castle, the size is relatively small to the
canvas due to distance being applied to the piece. There is some atmospheric distance
also applies, what causes the castle to be less clear in certain spots than others.
It creates a sort of fog through the cloud/air but only in the higher parts of the 
castle. There are horizontal lines with applied distance effect as it continues going across the painting.
Along with these lines it is dead greyish grass through fields and hills towards the castle. On these fields
and hills there are dead black trees everywhere. In the sky which is grey, there are 
gloomy clouds and grey skies. Which make it so much more emphasis on the ying and
yang crows through the skies, on top on multiple trees, and in groups flocking
together on the floor. The contrast of this majoritly grey environment is being
infiltrated by dark black creatures everywhere(the crows). What the final album will
cover is the movement of the crows to a certain direction. Towards a tall dark figure
off to the side. The character is small but assuming its a man made of crows.
