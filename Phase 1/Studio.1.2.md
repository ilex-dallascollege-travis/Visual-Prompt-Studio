artist name, ilex travis, album title, vamp anthem, genre, Heavy Metal / Gothic, Focus on dark stone architecture, high value contrast with deep shadows, jagged lines, and a sense of intense physical volume or mass. For value & space: The castle is becoming smaller and less clear with distance, atmospheric effect, and the strong light/dark contrast. Motion & Time, the movement of the crows toward the figure. The idea of repeated crows creating a directional flow connects well with the explanation of implied motion and repeated marks. Unity, Variety, & Balance, the repeated black crows can create unity while their difference positions and groupings create variety. The overall distribution of the castle, landscape, crows, and figure can also establish balance. Scale and Proportion, my distant castle being relatively small and the tall crow figure being more prominent being useful examples of scale. Focal Point & Emphasis, the tall figure made of crows is the focal point, line, value. scale, and placement all direct attention toward it. Pattern & Rhythm, the repeated crows are the strongest connection to this idea. The crows are designed to guide the viewer's eyes from across the canvas to the man made of crows. The strong vertical lines of the far away vampire castle, the size is relatively small to the canvas due to distance being applied to the piece. There is some atmospheric distance also applies, what causes the castle to be less clear in certain spots than others. It creates a sort of fog through the cloud/air but only in the higher parts of the  castle. There are horizontal lines with applied distance effect as it continues going across the painting. Along with these lines it is dead greyish grass through fields and hills towards the castle. On these fields and hills there are dead black trees everywhere. In the sky which is grey, there are  gloomy clouds and grey skies. Which make it so much more emphasis on the ying and yang crows through the skies, on top on multiple trees, and in groups flocking together on the floor. The contrast of this majorly grey environment is being infiltrated by dark black creatures everywhere(the crows). What the final album will cover is the movement of the crows to a certain direction. Towards a tall dark figure off to the side. The character is small but assuming its a man made of crows.

Glossary

Value- This is the way artists can add mood into a piece, by emphasizing certain parts by either add light or dark.

Space- Space within, apart or between parts of the piece

Unity- This is what makes all the different parts of an artwork feel like they belong together. This can happen through similar colors, textures, shapes, or an overall theme.

Balance- This is how the visual weight of different parts of an artwork is spread throughout the composition. It does not always have to be perfectly even, because one side can have more visual weight than the other on purpose.

Rhythm- Is the way a art piece can make your eyes wonder around the art, but more importantly the directioning, how your eyes follow through the artwork.
