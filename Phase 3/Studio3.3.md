project title:
Greek Dream

text prompt:
Create a promotional landing page for an exhibition titled “The Greek Dream,” opening in June, July, and August 2027, all summer. The subject matter is an ancient Greek coastal landscape in which three white-columned buildings climb a cliffside, from a sandy beach at the bottom up to a school at the top. Each level holds one adult citizen who shows Greek ideals of beauty and self-expression. Dolphins, fish, and other Mediterranean sea life appear in the water below.
The figures follow the Greek and Roman aesthetic of fit, attractive bodies, shown as natural, confident, and proud, with two different male and two different female body types. Each woman’s chest is covered by a cloth that wraps fully around her, thin enough that the natural shape of her body is softly visible, to express body positivity and acceptance of women’s bodies. 
Every figure has natural body hair and is not airbrushed. Their private areas are mostly covered by a small cloth held by a thin rope tied at the waist, with a hint of natural hair showing at the edges. This shows Greek forms of self-expression and identity, where the body is celebrated as part of who a person is.
In the bathhouse stands a very tall, wide-framed, thickly muscled Italian man with a light brown skin tone, brown-blue eyes, strong facial features, and a determined look. He has short dark hair with a middle part and a well-groomed goatee. He holds a strigil in one hand, with both arms raised out and up in a wide pose that shows his large torso, with extremely defined, thick abdominal and serratus muscles and a modest amount of natural armpit hair. A well-healed sword scar crosses his torso, which is not ugly, just a slightly different skin color for contrast.
In the school, a lean, bald, handsome man with green eyes stands on a stage teaching while students study. He is thinner than average but muscular, and he takes pride in his body and facial features. A small tattoo marks him as enslaved, and it stands out against his paler skin from being mostly indoors.
In a forest garden stands a petite, shorter woman with a fair, pale complexion, blue eyes, rosy cheeks, and freckles. She has long, wavy, slightly darker ginger hair in curtain bangs. She has a toned torso and abs, strong quads, thick sides of the thighs, and softer, curvier glutes, a toned back, average arms, and slightly strong forearms. A small, healed stab scar sits on her right shoulder for contrast.
On the cliff edge stands a taller, thicker woman with well-distributed weight, brown and gold eyes, and a light brown, olive skin tone with a racially ambiguous look. She has a freshly styled, short, dark black bob. Her body is soft and curvy, less toned, with a higher body fat percentage that shows in her abdomen. She wears seashell bracelets on her left ankle and has a healed second-degree burn on her right forearm from a hot pan. She looks out at people playing and lying on the beach below under a warm, sunny sky.



The style is American Regionalism, inspired by the smooth, precise realism, crisp outlines, and simplified forms of Grant Wood’s American Gothic, without copying the composition or figures of that painting.This page blends Greek Idealism, with its order, marble sculpture, and perfect proportions, with the contemporary value of Identity and Self-Expression, embracing diverse lived experiences.The idealized Greek body meets real bodies with freckles, scars, and tattoos, creating a clash between an ideal and reality that shows how the two eras differ. 
The layout uses layers of movement. We have directional lines used in the artwork horizontal, diagonal, and vertical. Horizontal lines of hills and cliffs guide the eye across and down the page, blending together as one natural landscape, with trails that lead from each building to the figure beside it. Diagonal wave lines at the bottom add movement, and they point in the same direction the cliff woman is facing. Strong vertical lines from the tall, upright pillars show strength, elegance, peace, and stability, and they stand for a society strong enough that people can be confident in their self-expression. Shape adds contrast too: the geometric forms of the columns and triangular roofs stand against the organic, curving shapes of the bodies, hills, and waves.
Contour lines give every figure and building a crisp outline, keeping the forms sharp and clear. Contrast comes from complementary colors, a warm orange sunlight against deep blue sea, which makes the figures stand out as focal points. Realistic detail adds more contrast: soft skin with shadows, shine, and sleekness, visible muscle lines and curves, a range of beige and brown skin tones, and small imperfections like freckles, red hair, rosy cheeks, dark elbows, scars, a burn mark, and the tattoo. The page includes the exhibition title at the top and the summer 2027 dates below it., no nudity allowed.
